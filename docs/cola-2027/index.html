2027 Social Security COLA calculator (3.6%)

SSA announced a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment for 2027. It starts with benefits payable to nearly 72 million Social Security beneficiaries in January 2027; increased payments to about 7.3 million SSI recipients begin on December 31, 2026.

This is a simple estimate (current × 1.036). SSA's own rounding and any Medicare Part B premium change are not included — the 2027 Part B premium had not been announced by CMS when we checked. Your COLA notice from SSA has your exact amount (SSA says notices go online in late November 2026 for my Social Security account holders who sign up by November 18, 2026).

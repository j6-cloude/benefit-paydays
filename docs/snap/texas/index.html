Texas HHS: households certified before May 1, 2023 keep their existing issuance schedule (based on when benefits were first certified) unless benefits had a break of at least six months and the household reapplied after May 1, 2023. Your award letter (TF0001) shows your day.

The same day applies every month. Never give your EBT card number or PIN to anyone who calls or texts you.

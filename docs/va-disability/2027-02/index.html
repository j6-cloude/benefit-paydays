VA disability payment date February 2027

For VA disability compensation, pension, DIC and survivors pension recipients. Regular rule: 1st business day of each month, for the previous month's benefits; if the 1st is a weekend or holiday, VA pays on the last business day before it.

VA does not publish a dated payment calendar. This date applies VA's published rule (benefits for a month are paid the first business day of the following month; earlier when that is a non-business day) to the OPM federal holiday calendar.

VA disability payment dates 2026–2027

VA says compensation, pension, education and survivors benefits are paid monthly; benefits for a month are normally paid on the first business day of the following month, and when that day is a non-business day or holiday, on the last business day before the 1st. VA does not publish a dated calendar, so the dates below apply that rule to the OPM holiday calendar. The December 2027 date is unconfirmed (see the December 2027 page).

SSI payment dates 2026–2027

SSA pays Supplemental Security Income on the 1st of each month. When the 1st is a weekend or federal holiday, SSI arrives on the business day before — so some months have two SSI deposits and the next month has none. That early deposit is next month's payment, not a bonus.

Social Security payment dates 2026–2027 by birthday

SSA pays retirement, survivors and disability (SSDI) benefits on a Wednesday chosen by your birth date: 1st–10th → second Wednesday, 11th–20th → third Wednesday, 21st–31st → fourth Wednesday. If you got benefits before May 1997, or get both Social Security and SSI, Social Security is paid on the 3rd. Any of these days that falls on a weekend or federal holiday moves to the business day before.
